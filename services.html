The Methodology

The ThndrStrm
Forecast

Every ThndrStrm engagement runs on the same proven framework — five phases that move from reading the environment to transferring the capability. Whether you engage for a month or a year, the sequence never changes. The depth does.

Phase 01

Read the Moisture

In meteorology: the raw material — water vapor that gives a storm something to work with.

Before touching strategy, take stock of what's actually in the room. What resources, relationships, and capacity exist? Most organizations underestimate what they already have to work with.

Phase 02

Find the Instability

In meteorology: what makes air want to rise — the gap between where things are and where they want to go.

Name the gap clearly enough that everyone in the room can see it. This is the breakthrough moment — the thing clients remember long after the engagement ends.

Phase 03

Create the Lift

In meteorology: the specific mechanism that forces air upward and gets the storm moving.

Find the specific action or decision that gets everything moving in the right direction. The leverage point reveals itself through iteration — not analysis. Bias toward action.

Phase 04

Build the Shear

In meteorology: changing wind speed and direction that organizes chaotic energy into a directed storm.

Build strategy around the team, the budget, and the timeline that actually exist. A plan that requires ideal conditions isn't a plan. It's a wish.

Phase 05

Complete the Circuit

In meteorology: lightning doesn't just happen in the cloud — it completes a circuit, discharging fully to the ground.

The goal of every engagement is to make outside help unnecessary. Leave the org sharper and fully capable of running without us.
